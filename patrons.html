Patrons of The Astorian

The Astorian exists because readers, writers, and patrons believe that good literature remains worth cultivating. In an age of haste and distraction, we are committed to publishing work built to endure: stories, poems, essays, and criticism marked by craftsmanship, beauty, and seriousness of purpose. Patron support allows us to compensate contributors, maintain our publication, and continue providing a home for literature with a proclivity for the old.

We extend our sincere thanks to the individuals listed below, whose generosity helps sustain our work.
