Spiritus Sanctus

By John Muro · April 2026

In tender fury come, ravage with fire, frond and stone make anew my heart and soul; quell, pilfer and crush the whole of mortal muscle, blood and bone. Cleanse this soul with the holy froth of undulant seas; the balm of astral dust, divine and luminous, both woven into this tattered cloth now a gilded garment of infallible design, fully sanctified. Glory be my salvation and sweet release from sin through your mercy, grace and everlasting peace.

John Muro, a two-time nominee for the Pushcart Prize and, more recently, for the Best of the Net Award, is a resident of Connecticut and a graduate of Trinity College.
