Watching the Goslings at Duck Pond

For my Father

By Michial Miller · September 2026

Five downy goslings float out to the center of Duck Pond paddling about their guardians, shepherded to the depths and dodging broken branches — some leafed, some bare — each navigates a fountain that sends crystal spray, arced, conical, slapping into the dappled surface, Hunter’s Green. I allow the ants safe passage over my body.

Michial Miller is a Southern Poet from Mississippi, currently residing in New Jersey.
