Submissions

  • What we read
    Fiction (prose and poetry), essays, and criticism.
  • Status
    Open. We are currently accepting public submissions for digital and print, compliant with our guidelines here. A portion will be donated to Episcopal Charities of New York. Accepted submissions will receive a stipend. If we are interested in publishing your work, you will hear from us within three weeks. Due to the volume of submissions, we can only reply to those whose work has been accepted.
